The African Apostolic Church (VaApostora VeAfrica) is a Christian denomination that originated in Zimbabwe. It is grounded in the teachings of the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ, as first established in Jerusalem.

The church recognizes the Holy Spirit as central to its guidance and formation.It upholds the authority of divine revelation and places emphasis on miracles, healing, deliverance, and apostolic order.

Our History

The name “The African Apostolic Church, VaApostora VeAfrica” was revealed in 1957 to Paul Mwazha of Africa when he heard the voice of the Holy Spirit.

At that time, the church had not yet been formed, and the words were received as prophetic utterances. From then on, Paul Mwazha’s ministry was marked by miraculous works—transforming lives, healing the sick, and delivering many from spiritual oppression, including witchcraft and demonic torment. In 1959, after receiving a direct and vivid instruction from the Holy Spirit, Archbishop Paul Mwazha formally established the African Apostolic Church.

The Calling

The Divine Commission

Prior to this, Paul Mwazha of Africa (the Arch-Bishop of The African Apostolic Church) had received his calling in 1951 when he heard the voice of the Lord saying, “who shall we send, who shall go for us?”.

He then went on to pray to God and saying, “I am here Lord, sent me to your people beginning in Africa first, then the rest of the world if be it your will Lord”.

The church today

The African Apostolic Church — where hearts are open, spirits are lifted, and love abounds.

While the name “The African Apostolic Church (VaApostora VeAfrica)” reflects the church's origin, its mission is universal. The church carries a divine mandate to reach all nations with the Gospel of Jesus Christ, inviting people of every race, culture, and background to share in the saving message of the Kingdom of God.

Spiritual reconciliation

The commissioning of Paul Mwazha of Africa has ushered a new era, of reviving the church of the olden days, tracing its roots back to the Apostolic traditions back in Jerusalem, thereby recreating the world though a true ordinance of God which is to be applied to all people of all nations, race and creed.

Our Mission

The mission of The African Apostolic Church is to re-establish God's Kingdom on earth by teaching individuals, families, and communities to transform their lives, cultures, and traditions in alignment with the Word of God. This is the gospel of Jesus Christ — a call to holiness, order, and divine purpose..

Our Values

We believe that living by God's Word transforms every aspect of life from how people dress, eat, speak, and interact, to how they worship and how they conduct themselves in every sphere of life Through this transformation, the Church helps humanity rediscover its original lost Godly culture: a life of obedience, humility, and purity according to the statutes and laws of God.
