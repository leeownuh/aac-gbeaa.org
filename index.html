Statement of Faith

We affirm the Holy Bible, as the inspired word of God and the basis for our beliefs. We believe in the Ten Commandments including the Holy Sabbath of the Lord (Exodus 20). We continuously strive to live like our Lord Jesus Christ, who came to fulfil the law. We voluntarily band ourselves together as a body of baptized believers in Jesus Christ, baptized in the name of, "the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit", and personally committed to sharing the good news of salvation to lost mankind in all communities that we live.

Our Belief

We believe in the believer's baptism as written in (Matthew 28:19; Acts 2:41) and the Lord's Supper as it appears in (Luke 22:19-20; 1 Corinthians 11:23-26) in the holy books of the bible.

Our affirmation

The servant of God Archbishop Ernest Paul Mwazha was commissioned by our Lord Jesus Christ to proclaim the gospel of Jesus Christ in Africa and the world over.
